# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students and self-taught developers learning data structures & algorithms (DSA) — typically
prepping for interviews or coursework. They arrive confused by a concept (how does a sort
*actually* move elements? what does a pointer rewire look like?) and want to *see* it, not
just read pseudocode. Context: laptop, focused study sessions, often dark rooms / late nights.

## Product Purpose
AlgoAnalyzer is an interactive algorithm visualizer. Pick an algorithm (sorting, searching,
stacks, linked lists), feed it input, and step forward/backward/auto-play through every
operation with the exact line of source code (Java / C++) highlighted in sync. Compare two
algorithms side by side. Success = the moment a concept "clicks" and the learner can predict
the next step before pressing play.

## Brand Personality
Focused, clear, quietly technical. Three words: **precise, friendly, hands-on.** It should feel
like a sharp study tool built by a developer who cares — not a flashy SaaS product or a
gamified ed-tech app. Confidence without noise.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient-text hero, eyebrow kicker on every section, identical
  icon-card grids, big hero-metric template).
- Gamified ed-tech (cartoon mascots, confetti, badges).
- Over-animated portfolio sites where motion fights the content.

## Design Principles
1. **Show, don't tell.** The product *is* visualization — the landing and UI should demonstrate
   motion and step-through, not just describe them.
2. **Earn every decoration.** Motion, color, and ornament serve comprehension or hierarchy, or
   they get cut. No reflexive eyebrows, gradient text, or decorative blobs.
3. **Calm dark surface, one confident accent.** Deep navy canvas, violet (#7c3aed) as the single
   brand signal; category colors (blue/emerald/orange) only where they carry meaning.
4. **Readable first.** Body text holds contrast; type identity (Bricolage Grotesque display +
   Outfit body) is actually loaded and doing work.
5. **Respect the reader.** Reduced-motion is a first-class path; reveals enhance already-visible
   content, never gate it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Dark default with a working light theme. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored across all landing
animations (no transforms/count-ups; content visible immediately). Aim WCAG AA for body text
contrast. Decorative SVG/graphics marked `aria-hidden`.
